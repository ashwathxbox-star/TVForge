# Compatibility

## Intended support

- Android TV 11 (API 30) and newer.
- Devices exposing Android TV/Leanback launcher activities.
- Physical remotes with D-pad and Select/Enter keys.

## Not currently supported or verified

- Jio set-top boxes (including JHS/JMS variants).
- Android TV devices below Android 11.
- Google TV and vendor-customised firmware beyond basic best-effort testing.
- Devices that prevent third-party launchers from being selected as Home.

## Notes

The launcher uses standard Android APIs. Manufacturers may restrict third-party Home apps, package visibility, or installation from unknown sources. Compatibility must be tested on each device family. The app's storage figure describes the app's accessible storage volume, not necessarily the entire device's user-data partition.
